MaleCNS CyberPet v0.1 - Windows

1) Make sure drive J: exists.
2) Double-click INSTALL_OFFICIAL_MALECNS.bat
3) Wait until it says MaleCNS verification completed.
4) Double-click RUN_FLY.bat
5) Q / Esc / close window = save and quit.

Persistent memory:
J:\FLY\save\pet_state.json
J:\FLY\save\memories.jsonl

MaleCNS data/cache:
J:\FLY\male_cns_data
